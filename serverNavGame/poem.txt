Steps:
1. Turn the stone 3 times in the closet and close the gate
2. Use the cue from the billiard room on the mirror
3. Walk to the piano in the ballroom, only on white tiles
4. Use the spade from the shed to dig in the yard




The closet keeps a sound I know,
a small thing turning, soft and slow.
It steadied me.  It always did
3 turns, and then I closed the lid.

The mirror showed me what I'd become.
I looked past it up above,
to a ledge the glass didn't show.
I took it when I should have let go.

That night the ballroom glimmered,
its glow a floorplan made of light.
The shadows called me, but I kept the glare.
Walked front and center, because it wanted me there.

When I dug into what lay underneath,
I felt it crawl into me through the grass
But this time it was different.
It was patient, deliberate, a deep mass,
settled where my thoughts used to be.
I called it comfort, it called me down.
